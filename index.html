Data Center

Private Intel administration. Only your admin account should see this section.

This is your CMS. You should not need to manually fill relationship tables. For example, saving a weapon with selected attachments can create the Weapon row and its Weapon_Attachments rows automatically. Camo assignments can similarly create the Camo_Weapon_Modes row and its challenges.
CONNECT ATTACHMENTS
HOW THIS WORKS

A camo is stored once, then its relationship to a weapon and mode is stored separately. That means the same weapon can have different camos/challenges for Multiplayer, Warzone and DMZ.

The final Supabase version will let you select existing weapons/camos from dropdowns instead of typing IDs.
OTHER ADMIN DATA

The production Data Center will include editors for Operators, Perks, Killstreaks, Equipment, Game Modes, Ranked Rules and News here. Community-owned records such as player profiles, friendships, votes, saved builds and camo progress are created through the normal app.
